'ON GRANDFATHERS'

- wood
- cardboard
- glazed ceramics
- acrylic

Made in collaboration with Luka Boduljak.
Exhibited in Paradise, The Hague as part of the
exhibition 'Where are you currently' vol.4.
Some grandfathers say they can still drive, even when they can't, and some tell the same stories, again and again, and some don't tell anything at all, but they leave something that speaks for them.
Yan and Luka tell the story of a made up someone, through objects that function chronologically as storytellers. Through scenography they turn fragments of the story into separate scenes, linked through reappearing motives.
