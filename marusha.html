'HAPPY BDAY MARUSHA'

Made in collaboration with Luka Boduljak.
Theater performance, shown in Korzo Theater
in March 2026, as part of the 'SOUNDSCAPES' class
at The Royal Academy Of Art in The Hague.

'HAPPY BIRTHDAY MARUSHA' is a theater performance that explores how anticipation could be built through dynamics of movement and sound on stage, and then be interrupted by something unexpected.
