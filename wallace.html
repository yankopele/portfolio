'WALLACE'

Animated film trailer.
Made in Blender.
'WALLACE' is an animated film that follows the story of a character, collaged and inspired by the early childhood drawings of my siblings. In the trailer, Wallace is seen in a series of different scenarios, that gradually become disturbing and questionable. Being a representation of a kid's spontaneous and unfiltered perception of the world, the character accepts everything that comes before his eyes with a wide smile on his face. Lacking any capability of critical reflection or moral distinction, as expected from a child, Wallace leaves this part to the viewers.
